# MD Fashions – Digital Marketing Campaign 💍
 
> A comprehensive multi-platform digital marketing strategy for an imitation jewellery shop — from zero online presence to 8,000+ impressions.
 
---
 
## About MD Fashions
 
MD Fashions is a retail store based in Viswanatheperi, Coimbatore dealing in imitation jewellery and fashion accessories. The shop caters to both purchase and rental markets — offering bridal jewellery sets, party wear, and daily wear accessories at affordable prices. Before this project, their online presence was minimal with little to no digital reach.
 
**Instagram:** [@md_fashions19](https://www.instagram.com/md_fashions19)  
**Website:** [mdfashionjewelry.wixsite.com/md-fashions](https://mdfashionjewelry.wixsite.com/md-fashions)
 
---
 
## Problem Statement
 
Despite offering quality products at competitive prices, MD Fashions faced:
- Limited brand awareness beyond the local area
- Inconsistent social media presence with irregular posting
- No targeted promotional campaigns to reach the right audience
- Minimal online traffic and customer engagement
- Zero analytics-based decision-making
---
 
## Objectives
 
1. Increase brand awareness through consistent digital campaigns
2. Establish and manage engaging social media pages on Instagram, Facebook, and YouTube
3. Drive traffic to the MD Fashions website using Google Ads and social media links
4. Create informative blog content on jewellery trends and styling tips
5. Run email marketing campaigns via Mailchimp for promotions and festive offers
6. Track and analyze performance metrics across all platforms
7. Evaluate customer behavior based on analytics and feedback
8. Recommend future strategies for sustained digital growth

## Implementation
 
### a) Instagram Marketing
- Created and optimized a business profile (`@md_fashions19`)
- Published reels, product photos, and festive collection videos
- Used targeted hashtags to boost organic reach
- **Result:** Grew from 9,437 → 9,902 followers during the campaign period
### b) Facebook Marketing
- Created a Facebook business page and linked it with Instagram
- Shared product updates, stories, and promotional offers
- Monitored performance through Meta Business Suite Insights
- **Result:** 5,200+ reach, 170 Facebook visits (+844.4%), 19 net new follows
### c) YouTube Marketing
- Uploaded jewellery styling and product demo videos
- Applied Video SEO — optimized titles, descriptions, and tags
- **Result:** 224 impressions, 12 views (+300% vs previous period), 1.3% CTR
### d) Google Ads
- Created keyword-targeted ad campaigns for jewellery-related search terms
- Used location-based targeting to attract local Coimbatore customers
- Researched high-volume keywords: "fashion jewelry" (50K/mo), "imitation jewellery" (50K/mo)
- **Result:** 300 impressions, 3.2% CTR, majority clicks from local female users
### e) Blogger
- Published 6 blog posts on topics like Temple Jewellery, Diamond Jewellery, Versatile Jewellery for Women, Bridal trends
- Embedded internal links to MD Fashions website and social media profiles
- **Result:** 417 all-time views, 335 views this month, organic discovery via SEO
### f) Mailchimp Email Campaigns
- Designed newsletters highlighting festive offers and new collections
- Sent campaigns to existing customers and new leads
- **Result:** 27% open rate, 4% click rate, top clicked link — MD Fashions website (71.43%)
